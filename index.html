Patient Guide

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, transparent answers to common patient questions about pregnancy, gynecology, and consultation modes.

1. How do I book an appointment?

You can book either an in-clinic visit or a video consultation directly through our website. Simply choose your preferred consultation type, select a suitable time slot, and confirm your booking.

2. What is the difference between a clinic visit and a video consultation?

Clinic visits are ideal for physical examinations, scans, procedures, and detailed evaluations. Video consultations are convenient for follow-ups, pregnancy guidance, reports discussion, PCOS management, menstrual concerns, and general medical advice.

3. Is video consultation safe and confidential?

Yes. All consultations are completely private and confidential. Your medical information and discussions are securely handled with professional confidentiality.

4. What health concerns can be discussed online?
You can consult for:
  • Pregnancy guidance & nutrition
  • PCOS/PCOD & irregular periods
  • Fertility & birth control advice
  • Reports review & follow-ups
5. When should I choose an in-clinic appointment?
An in-clinic visit is recommended if you need:
  • Physical examination or procedures
  • Ultrasound/scans
  • Severe pain or emergency evaluation
6. What should I keep ready before a video consultation?
Please keep the following ready:
  • Previous prescriptions or reports
  • List of current symptoms & medication details
  • Internet connection and a quiet environment
7. Can I consult during pregnancy through video consultation?

Yes. Many pregnancy-related concerns and follow-up discussions can be handled online. However, certain routine checkups and scans may still require clinic visits depending on your pregnancy stage.

8. Do I need to create an account before booking?

No complicated signup is required. You can quickly book your appointment using basic details like your name, phone number, and preferred consultation type.

9. What happens if I miss my appointment?

If you miss your appointment, you may contact our support team or clinic reception to reschedule based on slot availability.

10. How will I receive prescriptions after an online consultation?

After your consultation, the doctor can share your prescription and medical advice digitally through WhatsApp, email, or the patient portal.
